# what it is

A way for engineers to get better at their jobs, like the professional training sessions of footballers, tennis players, etc.

# why

Engineering now looks like what football was in the 1960s: talented people coming together for an outcome.

But the reality is that today, for each game they play, pro athletes do many hours of conditioning and training. 

I think that engineers would benefit from “time at the sw gym”, doing reps and learning new things from people exclusively adapt at building software.

This can be an internal position or an external paid service.
