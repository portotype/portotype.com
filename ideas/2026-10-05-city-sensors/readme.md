# what it is

Build a network of sensors for towns that can enable new AI applications.

# why

AI can now process Audio, Video and Image much better than before.

I propose a network of audio sensors in the city to measure noise and traffic, others for pollution, temperature, wind, sun exposure, sea and beach conditions.

Sensors are super inexpensive. The towns can get sensor data for free and private companies can analyze data in bulk, in a private way.
