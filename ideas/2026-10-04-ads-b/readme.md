# what this is 

Manufacture and sell a Plug & Play component for Autonomous Vehicles that transmits position and attitude.

It is an extension of ADS-B. What it is:  Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B) is an aviation surveillance technology that allows aircraft to continuously broadcast their precise position, altitude, and speed via satellite navigation.

# why

In 2026 are in the beginning of an explosion of autonomous vehicles. Military, research and commercial applications are fueling this growth. 

Naturally, there will be a need to coordinate trajectories of these devices on air, land, and sea.

So we will build a system that is plug and play and that allows the transmission of, cumulatively:
- position (inc altitude) 
- attitude 
- near term flight plan

The data can be inferred by the unit and/or supplied by the flight stack or onboard computer. 

Some of it may be encrypted and accessed only via keys.
