# what it is

An EU-wide operation for the distribution and servicing of robots and robotic components. Learn, then go into the component business too!

# why

A robotic revolution is imminent. Most advanced robots, humanoid or not, are not manufactured in Europe.

The brands (Unitree, etc) will need local operations to sell and service robots, including actually shipping the units, servicing them in and out of warranty, selling replacement parts.

the learnings will empower the player to learn and get ready for a future of consolidation, where key components become more streamlined and new specialty extras can be sold.
